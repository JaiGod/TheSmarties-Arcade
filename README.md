# TheSmarties Arcade

A Discord bot with games, study music and server tools.

## Commands

**Games**
- `/money` – idle money empire: work for cash, buy businesses that earn while you're away (up to 8h), grab pop-up bonuses before they vanish, claim a daily reward (streaks give more), prestige for gems (+10% income each). `/moneytop` for the leaderboard.
- `/mine` – cookie mining: smash cookies that shrink as their HP drops, buy 7 tiers of tools, enchant them (Sharpness, Fortune, Luck). Golden cookies give 10× crumbs, every 10th cookie is a MEGA cookie, and Sugar Rush doubles damage. `/minetop`.
- `/cookie` – cookie clicker. `/cookietop`.
- `/td` – co-op tower defense.
- `/story` – Station Kepler-9, a story with 6 endings.

**Study**
- `/study start` – joins your voice channel, plays study music on loop and runs a Pomodoro timer (music pauses during breaks). Choose the style (lo-fi, ambient, focus, classical), intensity (calm, deep focus, energetic), background sound (rain, café, white noise) and the timer lengths.
- `/study stop` – ends it.

**Tools**
- `/remind 1h30m take a break` and `/reminders` to see or cancel them.
- `/todo add` and `/todo list` – a personal checklist (only you see it).
- `/serverstats` – members, channels, and a 7-day message chart (counts only, never the text).
- Admins: `/rolemenu` (buttons that hand out roles), `/automod on|off|status` (switches on Discord's own spam and bad-word filters), `/modlog #channel` (logs deleted and edited messages).

## Running it

Double-click `start.bat`. Keep the black window open – closing it turns the bot off. After changing any files, close the window and double-click `start.bat` again.

If something goes wrong, the reason is written to `status.txt` and `errors.log` in this folder.

## Permissions

The bot needs extra permissions for some tools: **Manage Roles** for `/rolemenu`, **Manage Server** for `/automod`, and **Connect** and **Speak** for study music. Re-add it to the server with this link to grant them (it keeps all its data):

https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1558869308891865129&scope=bot+applications.commands&permissions=271666208

For `/rolemenu`, the bot's role must also sit **above** the roles it hands out (Server Settings → Roles, drag it up).

### Showing message text in /modlog (optional)

By default the log shows who deleted a message and where, but not what it said. To include the text:
1. Developer Portal → your app → **Bot** → turn on **Message Content Intent** → Save.
2. Add a line `MESSAGE_CONTENT=1` to the `.env` file and restart the bot.

## Good to know

- The music is generated by the bot itself from maths (synth notes, drums and shaped noise), so there are no music files and no outside service. It's simple, calm background sound, not a real recorded song.
- Progress is saved in `save.json`. Delete it to reset everything.
- After a restart, buttons on old game messages stop working – run the command again. Role menu buttons keep working.
